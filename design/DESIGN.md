# Design system — il0g Website (2/2)

Generated by the designer agent. This is the approved look of the product;
implement against it rather than inventing styling per ticket.

- Platform: **web**
- Stylesheet: `design/design-system.css` (14084 chars, 71 classes)
- Entry screen: `home`

## How to use this

1. Serve `design/design-system.css` as a real stylesheet and link it from your
   base layout. Do not paste its rules into templates.
2. Reuse the class names below. They already match the mockups.
3. The screens are **mockups**: their content is placeholder. Copy the
   structure and classes, never the data — render real records through the
   framework's templating.
