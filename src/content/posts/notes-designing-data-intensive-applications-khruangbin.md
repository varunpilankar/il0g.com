## 1. Append-Only Logs as the Universal Systems Primitive

Every time I revisit Martin Kleppmann's *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* before my university lectures, Chapter 9 ("Consistency and Consensus") stands out. Once you view filesystems (ZFS intent logs), databases (WAL), and distributed state machines through the lens of immutable append-only logs, half of systems engineering clicks into place.
